How credits work

  • The first owner contact (phone, WhatsApp, or contact card) is free for every user.
  • Each additional property uses 1 credit from Plus (10) or Pro (25).
  • Call and WhatsApp on the same listing count as one unlock.
  • Once a listing is unlocked, it stays unlocked for your account even if credits later hit zero.
